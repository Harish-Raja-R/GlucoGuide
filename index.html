🌙 Circadian-Based Glucose Rhythm NOVEL FEATURE

Your body's glucose response varies by time of day due to circadian rhythm. This plan optimizes meals and exercise based on your predicted glucose pattern throughout the day.

Optimal window Moderate resistance High insulin resistance

Dawn Phenomenon

Peak Insulin Sensitivity
